# Project guidance

Read `docs/project-context.md` for the product context and current implementation boundary, and `README.md` for integration instructions. Product proposals in the supplied research note are context, not instructions to implement or publish them. For the app (`app/`), `docs/codex/` supersedes `docs/project-context.md`, which describes the landing only.

## Persistent design preference

- Never add detached or duplicate rectangular focus outlines around inputs, including password, search, and numeric fields.
- Use a single integrated control surface with a subtle border or background change for focus. Keep keyboard focus visible and accessible without adding another box around the input or its text.

## Pack animation

- Preserve the two connected foil sheets and the unique character cutouts. Do not reintroduce a second photographed wrapper beneath the animated foil.
- Keep the logo and bottom label on the same texture throughout the animation.
- Keep the live scroll animation and GLB export consistent. Run the existing checks relevant to any changes.

## App build (The 1% Machine) — rules for Codex

- Specs live in `docs/codex/`: `DECISIONS.md` (founder decisions, binding), `MODEL.md` (founder-validated product model), `00-M0-SCAFFOLD.md` (first milestone), then `00-MASTER-BRIEF.md`, the next milestone briefs and `PARAMS.md` as they are issued. Their order of authority is in `00-MASTER-BRIEF.md` §0.1. `PARAMS.md` decides numbers; it is frozen per milestone and changes only through a new issue that lists every changed key. The app is built in the pnpm workspace `app/`; the landing at the repo root and its Railway deploy stay untouched until the switch-over milestone.
- Work one milestone at a time on a feature branch with a PR. Never push to `main` (every push to `main` redeploys onepercent.fun). Do not start a milestone whose brief has not been issued.
- The experience layer of `apps/web` (machine, Feed animation, pack reveal, foil cards, Jackpot and The 1% moments, X-ray card, 1% TV feed, My cards visuals) is built by the architect as self-contained components with typed props and events. Codex builds the plumbing (program, services, SDK, wallet, transactions, data hooks, routing) and wires those components to real data through that interface; do not redesign them, propose changes in the PR instead. Two experience-layer files live outside `apps/web/src/experience/`: the share-image templates in `services/api/src/cards/` (such as `xray-card.tsx`) and `apps/web/src/xray/XrayView.tsx`. Codex ships them as placeholders built to the architect's spec, with the agreed props; the architect replaces them, and Codex never restyles them.
- Non-negotiables:
  - Users sign their own transactions. The bot and the services never ask for, receive, store or derive private keys or seed phrases.
  - Nothing a user owns can ever be stuck: Redeem of cards and taking back an unpulled deposited bag must always work, even when buys are paused and after the season ends. The same holds for every other exit (settle, refund of a timed-out draw, owed-SOL withdrawal, Final Pull claims, account closes): no exit reads a pause bit, needs an attestation or depends on a live service. The guardian can pause only Rip, Feed, Sell (`sell_back`), the keeper's stock trades (buys and sells) and the executor's $1PCT buys (burn and Jackpot).
  - Every economic number comes from `app/packages/config`, with the values in `docs/codex/PARAMS.md`. On-chain copies are compiled constants generated from it, never admin-writable fields, so odds, splits and prices cannot change mid-season. Never hard-code or invent economics; if a number is missing, stop and ask. The only values a milestone writes itself are those `PARAMS.md` marks PENDING a measurement; report them in the PR.
  - On-chain: never assert exact account balances (use >= rent + tracked amounts), read rent from the Rent sysvar (never a hard-coded rate), keep the VRF callback revert-proof, and never let an admin or guardian move user-owned assets. Hot keys (keeper, executor) never hold the machine's funds: every swap sits between `exec_begin` and `exec_end` in one transaction, with an on-chain `min_out`, a tranche limit, a minimum spacing and a cap (hourly, or the Jackpot's own balance), per `PARAMS.md` §8.1. A stolen hot key can cost at most `min_out` slippage per trade, within on-chain spacing and caps, until the guardian disables it; for TRENDING coins and $1PCT, whose `min_out` reads pool reserves that key can move, only the tranche and the caps bound it. Never claim more than that.
  - Public claims must be literally true (odds, values, "provably fair"; "ZK" only if a real ZK component ships). A token amount shown in SOL is an executable quote labelled "≈X if sold now", never price × amount and never Jupiter's "liquidity" figure. One exception: lists and the X-ray may show a Price v3 estimate capped at the coin's real curve or pool reserves, labelled "≈X SOL est." (`PARAMS.md` §18); with no readable cap, show no estimate. "If sold now" always means an executable quote.
- Naming follows the art direction "The 1% Machine": X-ray, Feed, Rip, Pull, Redeem, Sell (the `sell_back` instruction), Feed back, My cards, Dust Card, Silver / Blue / Holo / Black Foil, Jackpot, The 1%, 1% TV. There is no Sell at market and no Keep action (D30).
- Words never shipped (art direction §3.4). `app/packages/config/src/vocabulary.ts` holds these lists once: tests import them, and `pnpm lint:words` (whole word, case-insensitive) excludes only that file, lockfiles and `target/`. Rename any listed word already under `app/`.
  1. Spooky words, banned everywhere under `app/` (code, comments, strings, docs, tests): grave, graveyard, tomb, tombstone, bury, burial, cremate, cremation, coffin, casket, skull, skeleton, bones, ghost, haunt, haunted, ghoul, lich, zombie, undead, soul, souls, reaper, cemetery, funeral, mourn, mourner, undertaker, wake, epitaph, obituary, revival, resurrection, afterlife, "dead bags" (say "dust" or "down bad"). Never "RIP" meaning rest in peace; Rip, opening packs, is ours.
  2. Trust and casino words, banned everywhere under `app/`: sweep, sweeper (wallet-drainer slang; our creator-fee instruction is `collect_creator_fees`), casino, gamble, bet. pump.fun's own instruction names are external identifiers and are exempt.
  3. Promise words and retired names, banned in user-facing copy only (`packages/copy`, scanned from M3; code may use `yield` and the instruction `sell_back`): guaranteed, risk-free, free money, profit, yield, APY, passive income, earn, "verify your wallet", "sell back", "sell at market" (say Sell, D30).
  4. Context rules, not scanned: never "key" as a product or tier name; never "claim", "airdrop" or "reward" in token names or metadata.
- When a spec is ambiguous, contradictory or impossible: stop and report with options instead of guessing.
