# 1% CRT modeling contract

An editable, smooth, ivory 1990s CRT monitor adapted from the user-supplied three-quarter reference. It floats without a stand. This is a volumetric reconstruction of the monitor; the screen artwork is a supplied image and its reactive television effect belongs to the web renderer.

- Outer facade: 4.2 wide by 3.5 high, with substantial continuous rounded bezel, recessed 4:3 convex screen and deep tapered rear shell.
- Required construction: shell split seam, stepped screen recess, bottom control panel with rocker and power button, green status LED, side/back/underside ventilation, rear power/video sockets, subtle geometric 1% mark.
- Materials: satin warm ivory plastic, darker cavity plastic, polished glass with image emission, dark graphite ports and muted metallic contacts.
- Runtime contract: root `CRT_Root`, front `CRT_Screen`, material `CRT_ScreenMaterial`. Blender X right, Z up, negative Y front. GLB X right, Y up, positive Z front. All moving behavior is root rotation controlled by JavaScript. Screen full-range UVs and no duplicate pane.
- Finish: polished smooth, not low-poly. Aim below 80k triangles, below 3 MB optimized, retain silhouette and manufactured bevels.
- Deliverables: deterministic Python builder, self-contained blend, lossless master GLB, Meshopt/WebP web GLB, transparent poster, dimensions and topology reports, six-view fresh import evidence.
- Review: compare the primary silhouette and curved screen against the supplied image. Inspect front, rear, side, top and hero views. Verify readability at about 750 px. Hidden rear details are inferred from CRT construction rather than claimed to reproduce an unseen original.
- Stages: references and contract; neutral proportion review; primary/secondary forms; topology and smoothness; UV/materials; physical detail and beauty lighting; export and fresh import validation. No approval gates.
