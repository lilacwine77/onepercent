# Token logos: pack X-ray and asset provenance

## Current use (9 October 2026)

The landing is Solana only. Hovering a booster reveals three coins inside it: **$WIF**, **$FONE** and **$JIMOTHY**, with the same cyan/purple X-ray shading. The printed wrapper artwork remains intact at rest. The ten Solana logos in `public/assets/tokens/` and their sources are listed in [`public/assets/tokens/SOURCES.md`](../public/assets/tokens/SOURCES.md); `node scripts/build-xray-coins.mjs` rebuilds the X-ray atlas from them.

Keyboard focus reveals the central pack. On touchscreens, tapping toggles the X-ray; **Explore** still opens the edition details. Dragging and horizontal scrolling remain available, vertical gestures continue to scroll the page, and reduced motion keeps the reveal static.

The [Robinhood Stock Token API documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-token-apis/) publishes a `logoUrl` for each asset. At verification, every selected Stock Token URL returned the same Robinhood feather image, so those URLs were unsuitable for visually distinguishing the examples. The company marks above come from Simple Icons, rather than Robinhood or the companies themselves.

[Robinhood describes its Stock Tokens](https://robinhood.com/rhj/stocktokens/) as tokenized debt securities that give economic exposure to underlying securities. They do not give holders legal or beneficial ownership rights in the underlying companies. The original strip identified these entries as Stock Tokens or tokens linked to companies, not company shares. pons and Artificial Inu are separate community tokens on Robinhood Chain, not Robinhood-issued Stock Tokens. Pons asks references to use lowercase `pons` and [link to its app](https://www.ponsfamily.com/) without suggesting an endorsement.

The former expanded row alternated community tokens and Stock Tokens: **PONS, BUN, NVDA, AI, UBIK, TSLA, BONER, AAPL, AUR, META**. It did not label tokens by market-cap bracket, publish prices or rankings, or claim that any token was funded in a Onepercent pack. BUN, UBIK and BONER were specifically requested; AUR added another project with a verifiable project identity and original image. The four new images were inspected directly before that integration.

## Historical logo-strip interaction

The removed strip moved continuously while visible. Hovering, focusing, clicking an arrow or tapping did not leave it paused. Holding a pointer or touch paused movement until release, including release outside the strip, cancellation or window blur. Mouse users could drag while holding. Keyboard users could hold Space to inspect and use the arrow keys to browse; releasing the key resumed movement. The system reduced-motion preference disabled automatic travel. A previous permanent pause toggle had been removed.

The strip retained native horizontal touch/trackpad scrolling. Its vertical overscroll was `auto`, with no blanket `data-lenis-prevent` attribute, so a vertical wheel gesture over a token continued to move the page. Infinite-loop duplicates were hidden from assistive technology. This describes the retired strip, not the active booster X-ray.
