# 1% homepage — content direction v2

## The idea in one breath

**Token holders load the pool. Players see the price and odds. One ripped pack reveals one token lot.**

This is the story the page must tell before explaining vaults or protocol economics. The foil and all-white Pepe make it memorable; the deposited tokens make it specific to 1%.

Use [Vibe Bevvy](https://vibebevvy.com/) as the page's sequence: quick brand promise, four tactile products, an image-led benefits section, a statement carousel, a large product turn, an editorial photograph, a short story, three editorial cards, a moving gallery, and a closing invitation. Each section below does the same job for 1%, without borrowing Vibe's wording or inventing customer praise.

## Writing guardrails

- Use large, legible ink or intentional brand color. No tiny gray microcopy, gray uppercase or monospace eyebrow labels, gray serial numbers, or pale gray divider lines.
- Do not say that packs can be bought, tokens deposited, odds verified, or prizes delivered today. The single concept line below the pack row carries that status without repeating it in every section.
- Do not invent APY, customer quotes, launch communities, token lists, guaranteed values, a native-token buyback, or a special probability implied by the name “1%.”
- The research note's `$10` example is an economic hypothesis, not a $10 product specification. Guide prices above are a creative lineup; the proposed mechanism must ultimately quote from actual inventory.
- Keep detailed RTP, LP profit, randomness, oracle and legal analysis in project documentation, not in the hero. The page should explain what a visitor can **see**, **choose** and **receive**.
- Generated scenes should contain no readable editorial copy. Keep names, prices and product explanations as HTML text; inspect every image at full size for invented signage or claims before using it.

## Delete from the rejected version

Remove `Pick your kind of thrill`, `The best part is the moment before`, `Own the moment`, `A collectible world`, `One big feeling`, `Follow the color`, `Feel the shimmer`, extra-character promises, and any purchase-like button without a real checkout. These lines explain neither token inventory nor why a 1% pack exists.
