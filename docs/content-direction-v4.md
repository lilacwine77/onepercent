# Onepercent — prizes and pools

Editorial direction, 1 October 2026. This supersedes the homepage direction in `content-v2.md`.

## Product brief confirmed by the founder

The landing page has two jobs: make people want the tokens inside a pack, and explain why token holders would deposit into its pool. **Each pack's top prize is 1% of a selected token's total supply.** Higher pack prices target tokens with higher market capitalizations. The name describes that top-prize size; the probability of drawing it is separate and must be shown for the actual inventory.

The desired prize universe includes tokens on Robinhood Chain, including PONS, Artificial Inu (AI), BUN, UBIK, BONER and AUR, alongside recognizable tokenized stocks. This does not establish a live prize list, a partnership with those projects, or access to any specific stock-token contract. A stock token is a token linked to an underlying security; a 1% token-supply prize must never be described as 1% ownership of the corresponding company.

The current repository is a visual concept. Payments, deposits, token custody, inventory, quotes, draws and settlement are not implemented. Show that once in a clear sentence beside the proposed product lineup, and keep interactive actions explicitly previews. The landing should lead with the product idea.

## Editing and presentation rules

- The two paths remain obvious at every scroll depth: receive tokens through packs, or deposit tokens into a pool.
- Use `Robinhood Chain` in explanatory copy so third-party tokens are not described as Robinhood-issued tokens. The current short hero `Get RH tokens.` is clarified immediately underneath.
- Never claim that 1% is the win probability, that every outcome is a 1% allocation, or that a stock-token prize confers 1% ownership of its underlying company.
- Do not invent target market caps, prize dollar values, odds, live balances, returned yields, partner status or token eligibility.
- Keep the moving logo row about proposed prizes; avoid the visual language of customer/partner endorsements.
- Use no tiny gray microcopy, gray uppercase labels, serial numbers or pale horizontal rules. Important conditions belong in readable ink within the content hierarchy.
- The user rejected the newly proposed bronze lineup. The active order is **silver / blue / signature holographic / black**.
