## Processing

Each logo is cropped to a centred square, resized to 384 × 384 (never enlarged: POPCAT stays at its 200 px source), and encoded as WebP with sharp (`quality: 90`, `effort: 6`). Nothing is redrawn.

## Where they are used

The pack X-ray shows `wif`, `fone` and `jimothy`. `node scripts/build-xray-coins.mjs` rebuilds `public/assets/pack-xray-coins.svg` and `pack-xray-coins.webp` from these files; pass three other slugs to swap the coins. The "How dust pays." section shows `ansem` (previously `fwog`). The other logos are not on the page.
